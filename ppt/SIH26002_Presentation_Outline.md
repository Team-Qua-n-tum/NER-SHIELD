# SIH26002 Presentation Outline
**Project: NER-SHIELD**

## Slide 1: Title
- **Team Name:** Quantum
- **Problem Statement:** SIH26002 (AI-based smart logistics and accessibility intelligence platform for NER)
- **Tagline:** Resilient, AI-Driven Supply Chain Intelligence for the North East

## Slide 2: The Challenge (Problem Context)
- Rugged terrain, extreme weather, frequent landslides in the NER.
- Logistics delays, opaque supply chains, lack of real-time hazard data.

## Slide 3: Our Solution (NER-SHIELD)
- A Unified Geospatial Intelligence Platform.
- **Key Features:**
  - Real-time Field Incident Reporting
  - AI-Driven Hazard Risk Assessment
  - Dynamic ETA and Route Optimization
  - Offline-first resilient architecture

## Slide 4: Technical Architecture
- **Frontend:** React, Vite, Leaflet GIS mapping.
- **Backend:** FastAPI, Python, PostGIS.
- **AI/ML Engine:** Scikit-Learn (Risk & ETA Models).
- **Resilience:** Fallback in-memory datastore (Demo Mode), resilient API client.

## Slide 5: The AI Advantage
- **Risk Model:** Predicts landslide/blockage probability using terrain slope, soil type, and rainfall data.
- **ETA Predictor:** Dynamically adjusts travel times factoring in vehicle type and active road conditions.

## Slide 6: Demo Overview
- Show the Operations Dashboard.
- Live Incident Reporting simulation.
- AI Route Replanning in action.

## Slide 7: Future Scope & Scaling
- Integration with satellite imagery (ISRO Bhuvan).
- Expanded IoT sensor networks for real-time bridge health.
- Deployment across other challenging terrains (Himalayan belt).
